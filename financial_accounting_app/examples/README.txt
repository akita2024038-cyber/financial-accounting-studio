Financial Accounting example workbooks
======================================
financial_accounting_task.xlsx  : original blank student assignment
financial_accounting_key.xlsx   : original complete solution (formulas)
demo_30_variants.zip            : 30 student task files + 30 solutions + combined answers
demo_all_answers.xlsx           : 90 calculated answers across 30 variants

Example sheets: Balance Sheet and Income Statement.
Input candidates: Balance Sheet!B4, Balance Sheet!B5,
                  Income Statement!B4, Income Statement!B5.
Answer rules: Balance Sheet!B6, Balance Sheet!B7, Income Statement!B6.
Accounting constraint in the demo: Balance Sheet!B7 = Balance Sheet!B4.
Formula examples: =B4-B5, =SUM(B5:B6).

These are educational synthetic data only; not real company financial statements.
